Privacy Policy

MealPlanatic helps you plan meals, track your pantry, build grocery lists, and compare store prices. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, and password (stored securely by our auth provider).
  • Profile: optional profile photo, home ZIP, household size, and dietary notes you enter.
  • Kitchen data: pantry items, recipes you save, meal plans, and grocery lists.
  • Location (approximate): ZIP code and coordinates you save for nearby store search — not continuous GPS tracking unless you choose to use device location features.
  • Photos: optional pantry shelf photos, price-tag photos, and profile avatars you upload.
  • Community prices: store deals and prices you submit for other shoppers (may be kept after account deletion in anonymized form — see below).
  • Usage: basic app diagnostics and error logs needed to keep the service running.

How we use information

  • Sync your kitchen data across devices when you sign in.
  • Rank recipes against your pantry and build your grocery list.
  • Find stores and compare prices near your saved ZIP.
  • Run AI photo scanning (Google Gemini) on images you choose to upload, to detect pantry items or read shelf tags.
  • When you import a recipe from a link, we send that URL (and for some sites, page text or a YouTube video reference) to Google Gemini to extract recipe details for your private collection.
  • Improve reliability and security of the app.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, and server functions) to host and process your data. Photo scanning sends image data to Google Gemini only when you start a scan. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app: open your avatar → Account → Delete account. That removes your profile, pantry, meal plans, grocery lists, saved stores, and personal recipes, and deletes your auth account. Optional scan and avatar files in storage tied to your account are removed as part of that process.

Community store prices you reported may remain visible to other users after deletion, but without your name or account link (reporter identity is cleared).

Children

MealPlanatic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
